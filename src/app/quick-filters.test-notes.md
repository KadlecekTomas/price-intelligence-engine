# Quick filter acceptance scenarios

- Category chip toggles on/off and triggers search immediately.
- Budget chip uses the existing deterministic maxPrice URL override.
- Apparel sizes show S–XXL; shoe categories show 41–45.
- Color and material chips are mutually toggled per group.
- Deal/price/quality priorities are reflected in deterministic filters.
- Quick-filter state remains shareable in the URL.
- Advanced filters stay available for sizes such as 43,5 and W32/L32.
- Mobile rows scroll horizontally instead of overflowing the viewport.
